01 · Scope

What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to VTera's public website and to personal information you voluntarily provide when you contact VTera through email or other direct business correspondence.

It does not describe how future pilot, customer, or enterprise platform data may be handled. Those arrangements may be governed by separate agreements, security terms, data-processing terms, or customer-specific requirements.

02 · Information we receive

Information you choose to send us

VTera does not currently provide public user accounts or a website contact form. If you choose to email us, we may receive information contained in your message.

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your company, role, or other professional information you choose to include.
  • The contents of your message and any files or information you attach.
  • Information related to a potential pilot, partnership, investment, support request, or other business inquiry.
Clicking a mailto: link does not itself send information to VTera. Information is received only if you choose to send the email.
03 · Use

Why we use information

Information you send to VTera may be used to:

  • Respond to your inquiry or request.
  • Discuss potential pilots, partnerships, investment, employment, suppliers, or other business relationships.
  • Provide support or follow up on matters you raise with us.
  • Maintain appropriate business correspondence and records.
  • Protect VTera, its systems, and its legal rights where reasonably necessary.
  • Comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.
04 · Sharing

We do not sell personal information

VTera does not sell personal information received through this website or through direct correspondence. Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary to operate the website or business, respond to your request, work with service providers acting on our behalf, comply with law, protect legitimate rights, or where you have authorized the disclosure.

05 · Technical data

What may happen automatically

VTera does not currently use advertising trackers, public user accounts, or behavioral advertising on this website. The site may, however, rely on hosting infrastructure and external resources that necessarily process basic technical request information, such as IP address, browser details, timestamps, or requested resources, as part of delivering the site.

The website may also store a theme preference locally in your browser so your light or dark mode choice can be remembered. This preference is used for interface functionality rather than advertising.

06 · Retention

We keep information only as long as it remains useful or necessary

Business correspondence and related personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, maintain relevant business records, preserve the history of a relationship or discussion, resolve disputes, or satisfy applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods can differ depending on the nature of the correspondence and the reason the information was provided.

07 · Your rights

You can contact us about your information

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may have rights relating to personal information held about you, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or cessation of certain processing, or withdrawal of consent where consent is the relevant basis.

If you would like to make a privacy-related request, contact help@vtera.co. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

08 · Security

Reasonable protection, without unrealistic promises

VTera takes reasonable steps appropriate to its current stage to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or loss. No internet transmission, email system, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so this policy does not promise absolute security.

09 · Changes

This policy will evolve with VTera

VTera may update this Privacy Policy as the website, company, services, legal requirements, or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
