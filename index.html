Most AI platforms force a deployment model that doesn't fit every business. Legacy enterprise systems are priced for Fortune 500 budgets and built for a different regulatory world. Neither works for a mid-to-large MENA operator sitting on invoices, shipment logs, customs records and contracts that were never meant to leave the building.

VTera structures enterprise data and deploys AI agents that produce real-time, auditable decisions · with full data sovereignty as the foundation, not a feature bolted on afterward.
