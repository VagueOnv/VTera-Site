Decision flow
The problem

Your systems know what happened. Your business still has to decide what happens next.

ERP, TMS, WMS, carrier systems, customs platforms, spreadsheets, documents, and external data each contain part of the picture.

The data is fragmented. The decision is not.

Critical operational decisions often live between systems. A delay can involve a shipment, carrier, route, customs event, contract, SLA, inventory position, and customer commitment at the same time.

VTera sits above those systems and creates a unified decision environment, so the organization can evaluate what matters, understand the tradeoffs, and determine what should happen next.

Architecture

VTera operates above the systems you already depend on.

It is designed to become the decision layer between enterprise information and enterprise action, rather than another system of record.
